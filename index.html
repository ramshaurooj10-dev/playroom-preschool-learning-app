Playroom – Preschool Learning Games & Activities for Kids

Playroom is an interactive educational learning application designed specifically for preschool children ages 3 to 6, parents, and early childhood educators.

Interactive Preschool Learning Activities

  • ABC Phonics & Letter Recognition: Interactive alphabet games, letter tracing, and phonics sounds.
  • Counting & Early Math: Number learning 1 to 10, balloon counting, size sorting, and early subtraction concepts.
  • Colors & Shapes: Color recognition, color mixing, shape matching, and guided worksheet coloring.
  • Matching & Observation: Find hidden objects, memory card matching, shadow matching, and odd one out.
  • Animals & Healthy Habits: Animal food matching, feeding animals, healthy food sorting, clean room, and daily routines.
  • Traffic Light & Road Safety: Color recognition teaching Red = Stop, Yellow = Slow, and Green = Go.
